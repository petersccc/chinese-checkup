Only to send you the personalized overview and recommended next step you asked for, and, if you go on to book a discovery call, to prepare for and follow up on that conversation.

For sending your personalized overview: your explicit consent, given through the checkbox shown at the point you submit your email. You can withdraw this consent at any time, see Your rights below. Withdrawing it does not affect anything already sent to you before that point.

For preparing and following up on a discovery call you choose to book: this is necessary to take steps you've asked for ahead of a potential working relationship, not something covered by the checkbox itself.

Whether AI processes your answers

No AI system processes your individual answers when you use this tool. Your result comes from a fixed scoring calculation, and the written result you see is selected from a set of texts that were written and reviewed by a person in advance, based on which two sections your score identifies as strongest and weakest. No AI system receives or analyzes your specific answers at the time you use the tool.

  • ClickUp, where your email and answer overview are stored if you submit them

Each of these acts as a data processor on our behalf, under its own data processing agreement.

Yes. Cloudflare, ClickUp, and Calendly are all US based and process data there. Each relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses, the transfer safeguard recognized under GDPR, and Cloudflare and Calendly are also certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework as an additional basis. These terms are already built into each provider's standard agreement, not something negotiated separately.

How long we keep your data
